Turn an ESPHome voice device into a natural voice interface for your Homey. Speak normally to control lights, thermostats, locks and everything else in your home, set timers and alarms, play music, and get quick answers to everyday questions. There is no rigid command syntax to learn, and the conversation stays open, so you can ask a follow-up without repeating the wake word. The assistant speaks your language — English, Dutch, German, French, Italian, Swedish, Norwegian, Spanish, Danish, Russian, Polish and Korean.

You choose the AI behind it: a low-latency cloud engine from OpenAI, Google or Mistral, or your own self-hosted services, so that no audio ever leaves your network. Extras such as web search, music and shopping lists can be switched on when you want them and stay out of the way when you don't.

Smart locks: locking by voice always works, but unlocking is off by default — anyone within earshot can talk to the device, so you must first enable "Allow unlocking by voice" in the app settings. Even then only one lock can be unlocked per command.

Supported devices, setup instructions, Flow cards and all the technical details are in the GitHub repository and the Homey Community topic linked below.
